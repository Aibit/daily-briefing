🇪🇺 Spain / EU Regulation

DSA Is Live — But Has No Rule on AI-Generated Creators. Spain Is the Test Case.

The EU's Digital Services Act (DSA) is now in full effect across all platforms serving European users, requiring transparency in content moderation, algorithmic systems, and advertising. However, the DSA has no specific provision governing disclosure for AI-generated influencers or synthetic creator personas. Barcelona's The Clueless is operating Aitana Lopez — a fully AI-generated influencer landing paid brand deals — in a regulatory grey zone. EU consumer protection rules require "clear and prominent" disclosure of commercial intent, but what counts as a "person" whose identity must be disclosed remains untested.

Why it matters: Spain is ground zero for this regulatory collision. As Barcelona-based AI creator agencies grow, the EU will almost certainly clarify DSA disclosure requirements for synthetic creators in 2026 or 2027. Brands working with human creators through compliant tools like hatchet.cool could use this as a positioning advantage as regulation tightens.
