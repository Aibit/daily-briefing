🇪🇺 Spain & EU Regulation

DSA Framework

The Digital Services Act: Active Enforcement Is Now the Operating Reality for EU-Facing Platforms

The EU's Digital Services Act has moved fully from legislation to enforcement: it now governs all online services used by European citizens — social networks, marketplaces, app stores, travel, and accommodation platforms. The DSA requires platforms to minimise exposure to illegal and harmful content, provide transparency in content moderation, offer algorithmic alternatives, and undergo independent audits. The European Commission has confirmed no slowdown in enforcement through 2027, with the DMA running in parallel for gatekeeper platforms.

Any creator or gaming platform operating in Spain or the broader EU market (including any brand running influencer campaigns targeting EU audiences) must treat DSA compliance as live cost of business — algorithm transparency, minor safeguards, and content moderation documentation are the Commission's primary audit targets this year.
